# Poisson Deconvolution of Delayed Death Counts — LinkedIn Projects entry

## Project name
Poisson Deconvolution of Delayed Death Counts

## Description
Team project reconstructing the timing of fatal infections from delayed English hospital death counts. Contributed bootstrap inference, visualisation and the write-up within a three-person team.

Used a positive B-spline curve, an assumed infection-to-death delay distribution, penalised Poisson likelihood and smoothing selection. An independent review implementation checks the archived equations on 150 daily counts, evaluates 50 smoothing candidates and performs 200 fixed-smoothing bootstrap refits.

The reconstructed peak precedes fitted deaths by 20 days, conditional on the chosen delay model. Analytical gradients agree with numerical checks. The curve concerns infections leading to the recorded deaths, not all community infections; the bootstrap bands are pointwise and do not include delay-model uncertainty.
