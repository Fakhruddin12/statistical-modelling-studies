# Bayesian Multilevel Modelling of Recorded Associations — LinkedIn Projects entry

## Project name
Bayesian Multilevel Modelling of Recorded Associations

## Description
Academic group coursework specifying three Bayesian logistic models for associations in recorded stop-and-arrest data. Compared fixed-effect structure with precinct-specific intercepts and time-of-day slopes, then added a recorded weapon indicator.

The source includes informative historical priors, leave-one-out predictive comparison and posterior predictive checks. The portfolio review clarifies the distinction between within-precinct and new-precinct prediction and adds explicit HH:MM/HHMM parsing with input validation.

This is currently a methods and code-audit project: original workbooks, prior-fit objects and posterior/LOO artifacts are missing, so no reproduced coefficients, model ranking or convergence result is claimed. Associations are descriptive and do not establish causal demographic effects or individual criminality.
